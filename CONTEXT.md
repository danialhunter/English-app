# EtonHouse English Tracker

The language used to describe individual English practice, lesson completion and parent progress reporting.

## Language

**Lesson**:
A set of five vocabulary targets and one sentence target for individual practice.
_Avoid_: Week, exam

**Scheduled week**:
The week to which a child's lesson is assigned, even when the child completes that lesson earlier.
_Avoid_: Practice date, lesson

**Knowledge mark**:
A teacher's recorded assessment of one vocabulary or sentence target: unassessed, needs practice, or knows it.
_Avoid_: Practice completion, exam result

**Knows it**:
A knowledge mark for an independent response to a normal question or picture prompt, accepting age-appropriate pronunciation rather than requiring imitation of the teacher.
_Avoid_: Repeated after the teacher, practice finished

**Needs practice**:
A knowledge mark for a target the child has not yet demonstrated independently, including a response that only repeats the teacher's supplied answer.
_Avoid_: Unassessed, failed exam

**Practice finished**:
The teacher's explicit confirmation that a practice session has ended; it does not itself mean the child knows every target.
_Avoid_: All known, lesson completed

**Lesson completed**:
A lesson for which all five vocabulary targets and the sentence target are marked as known and the teacher has explicitly finished the practice session.
_Avoid_: Practice finished, all known

**Completed early**:
A lesson completed before its scheduled week, with that completion also recognised in the scheduled week.
_Avoid_: Skipped, automatically finished

**Exam ready**:
A child whose teacher has explicitly finished practice for every lesson assigned to the assessment month, regardless of whether every target is known.
_Avoid_: All known, month ended

**Absent—exam pending**:
A teacher-confirmed absence with an outstanding monthly exam, shown separately in class information but not exempt from the child's own exam requirement.
_Avoid_: Passed, failed, withdrawn

**Monthly exam pass**:
A completed monthly assessment meeting its applicable scoring rule; the newly agreed rule requires at least 80% overall and a strict majority correct in each of vocabulary and sentences.
_Avoid_: Practice finished, class advancement

**Individual advancement**:
A child's permission to begin the next month's lessons after passing their own required monthly exam. Class results are informational and do not block a child who has qualified.
_Avoid_: Class advancement, every child passed

**Assessment coverage**:
The proportion of children in the month's class calculation who have finished a monthly exam, counting each child once rather than counting attempts.
_Avoid_: Pass rate, number of attempts

**Class pass rate**:
The proportion of non-absent children in the month's class calculation who have passed their monthly exam.
_Avoid_: Assessment coverage, average exam score

**Retest**:
A fresh assessment of the same complete short exam sample after targeted practice, kept as a separate attempt alongside previous results.
_Avoid_: Correcting an old result, testing only missed items

**Later review**:
A short check of previously practised targets, recorded separately from the original lesson or exam. A later difficulty does not erase an earlier achievement or revoke an exam pass.
_Avoid_: Regrading, exam correction

**Exam correction**:
A dated, reasoned correction identifying a genuinely mistaken completed exam record while preserving the original attempt. The child needs reassessment before further advancement.
_Avoid_: Later forgetting, deleting history

**Future goal adjustment**:
A recorded change to the expected response for an unstarted future lesson. It does not change the expectations or grades attached to earlier learning or a started exam.
_Avoid_: Regrading, lowering past results

**Newcomer catch-up assessment**:
An individual assessment of a newly joining child's remaining assigned lessons for the month, separate from the class percentage calculation until the following month.
_Avoid_: Class exemption, automatic pass

**Withdrawn child**:
A child who has left the class and no longer counts towards outstanding class assessment requirements, with their historical records retained.
_Avoid_: Absent child, erased record

**Parent progress report**:
A report for one child over a teacher-selected period, showing what the child has learned and how their learning has changed.
_Avoid_: Class summary, backup
