# EtonHouse English Tracker 2.5.0

## A simpler teaching day

- Choose a child, mark **Needs practice** or **Knows it**, then press **Finished**. Marking every item known does not finish the lesson for you.
- Practice, review, exams and parent reports have clear, direct entry points. Occasional class and backup tools stay separate from the lesson.
- No separate understanding observations or With help buttons. Each target has one assessment.
- The five words and one sentence stay central; optional notes and teaching resources do not crowd the daily workflow.

## Progress without confusion

- A child can complete lessons early within the unlocked month. Completed lessons retain their assigned week and recorded history.
- Exam alerts appear at the genuine checkpoint, not after every earlier session. A child's own passing exam unlocks their next month; classmates' results do not hold them back.
- New exams use the shorter fixed sample, with at least 80% overall and a strict majority correct in both vocabulary and sentences. Every selected item must be checked, and the month's assigned practice sessions must be finished.
- Unfinished exams retain their saved answers and scoring format. You can pause and resume. Completed older exam results are not regraded.
- Later review records what the child remembers now without erasing earlier learning or automatically cancelling a passed exam. Corrections to an actual recording mistake retain an audit trail.
- Parent Excel reports have two separate progress graphs (words and sentence structures), learned examples, later review and a practical next step. They do not present unsupported separate ability scores.

## Keep your records

Install over the previous app under the same computer user account. The original storage locations and application identities are unchanged. The app creates `Backups/before-version-2.5.0.json` before upgrading existing data, without overwriting that safety copy on later reinstalls.

Save a full JSON backup in the old app before updating and keep it somewhere separate from the computer. Reinstalling is not a substitute for a backup. A new computer starts empty; use Restore a backup to move your class. Excel reports cannot restore the database.

Version 2.5.0 uses data schema 7. Update every computer that restores a new backup and do not downgrade. Version 2.4.2 refuses the new format instead of removing newer fields.

## Installation

- **Mac:** macOS 13 or later, Intel or Apple Silicon. Open the DMG, quit the old app, then drag EtonHouse English Tracker into Applications and choose Replace.
- **Windows:** 64-bit Windows 10 or later. Run the Setup EXE. The installer retains existing application data.

The Mac build is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarized. Windows is unsigned. Your computer may show an unidentified-developer or unknown-publisher warning. Do not disable system-wide security protections. The Windows installer is cross-built on macOS; a real Windows installation has not been tested here.
