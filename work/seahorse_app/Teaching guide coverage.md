# EtonHouse teaching guide coverage

The app now includes the complete original **A Teacher's Guide to English Teaching** (44 pages), with a searchable library and age-specific teaching companions for Pre-Nursery (2–3), Nursery (3–4), K1 (4–5) and K2 (5–6).

The existing 216 weekly lessons are preserved exactly, including their lesson identifiers, sequence, vocabulary, sentences and source pages. Their content was compared with the previously delivered Mac 2.0 installer; the lesson data is identical. Your recorded weeks keep their original lessons.

## What is available

| Area | Available in the app |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly plan | 216 lessons, each with five vocabulary entries and one sentence |
| Topic vocabulary | 1,072 source entries in the weekly plan; 1,096 terms in small library practice sets |
| Sentence structures | All 140 previously indexed grammar-column structures, individually selectable |
| Listening and speaking | 152 additional functional examples, individually selectable, with the guide's context and practice guidance |
| Grammar | Age-appropriate guidance, pronouns, possessive adjectives, demonstratives, prepositions, singular/plural forms, sentence patterns, tenses, modal verbs and conjunctions |
| Phonics | All seven printed sound groups, alternative spellings, blending, consonant blends, the eight teaching steps and five reading/writing skills |
| Sight words | All five printed lists: Pre-primer 40, Primer 52, 1st Grade 41, 2nd Grade 46, 3rd Grade 41 — 220 entries altogether |
| Reading and writing | Age-specific practice companions, guided-reading expectations, book handling, story discussion, word books, journals, writing purposes and punctuation |
| Songs, rhymes and teaching guidance | Age-specific companions plus all original lyrics, routines, book-sharing tips and guidance in the complete PDF and searchable page text |

The library has **647 selectable entries**. The full source pages remain available for details that are better read in the original layout. Teaching companions are clearly marked summaries; they do not replace the source text. External books, workbooks, audio and websites referred to by the guide are not part of the supplied document and are not bundled.

## Why the vocabulary totals differ

The earlier app retained grouped phrases as one printed entry. The library separates some of those into easier-to-use terms:

| Level | Weekly source entries | Library terms | Explanation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Pre-Nursery | 210 | 214 | “pets (dog, cat, bird, rabbit)” becomes five terms |
| Nursery | 271 | 287 | The afternoon/evening/night greetings and opposite adjective pairs are separated |
| Kindergarten 1 | 328 | 330 | Train, bus and taxi station are separate terms |
| Kindergarten 2 | 263 | 265 | Taxi/bus driver and refrigerator/fridge are separated |
| Total | 1,072 | 1,096 | These are source-entry counts, including repeats across topics and ages |

The difference of 24 was a counting convention, not 24 missing words. The substantive gaps in the earlier version were the functional-language examples, phonics, sight words and broader literacy/grammar guidance. These are now usable from the library.

## Classroom wording and source fidelity

The original PDF is kept unchanged. A few printed examples already had clearer classroom versions in the weekly plan. The library now identifies both the original and classroom version:

- Page 3: “What is your name? - XXX.” → “What is your name? - My name is ___.”
- Page 15: “I like funny book.” → “I like a funny book.”
- Page 15: “Tony have cat.” → “Tony has a cat.”
- Page 16: “The dog play(s).” → “The dog plays.”
- Page 32: “Which be/do...? Which one...is/do...?” → “Which ... is ...? / Which one does ...?”
- Page 33: “Why...? / Why...is important?” → “Why ...? / Why is ... important?”
- Page 35: “I am going to play slide.” → “I am going to play on the slide.”

The truncated vocabulary “rolling pi” is displayed as “rolling pin”. Functional-language punctuation and placeholders are normalised for classroom use. The repeated “oo” and “th” positions in the phonics list are retained because they represent different sounds. Repeated pronouns and vocabulary are retained where they occur in different source contexts.

## Verification

The audit used the original 44-page PDF from the Teaching folder, not just the previous extraction. All 1,072 weekly vocabulary entries and 140 sentence structures were checked against the corresponding PDF columns, allowing the documented number-range expansions and classroom wording changes. The five sight-word lists were compared word for word and in order with freshly extracted source text. The phonics sequence and sight-word pages were also visually inspected.

Automated checks confirm every library entry has a valid age level and source page, every vocabulary/sight-word practice set has at most five terms, all 44 pages are indexed, the bundled PDF matches the original file, and the 216 existing lessons are unchanged.

The guide emphasises individual development and observation. Pre-Nursery responses may be words or gestures; formal systematic phonics and independent whole-rhyme singing are not required at that stage. The app's practice checks should support those expectations and the teacher's judgement.
